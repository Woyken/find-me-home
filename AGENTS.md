## General guidelines

Avoid `as` type casting. Reuse types and be specific. Inferred types are best types.
If you see messages `[STRICT_READ_UNTRACKED]` `[NO_OWNER_EFFECT]` `[REACTIVITY_HALTED]` always treat it as error. Must be fixed.
