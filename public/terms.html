Find Me Home terms of use

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Find Me Home is a household decision-support tool. Its route durations, distances, suggested departure times, property facts, and automatic checks are estimates and may be incomplete, delayed, or wrong. Verify important information independently. Do not rely on the app for safety-critical navigation or to guarantee an arrival time.

Google Maps content

The on-demand driving estimate uses Google Maps Platform. Your use of Google Maps content is subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Do not copy, scrape, bulk-download, or redistribute the displayed route estimate.

Availability

The service is provided as available, without a promise that any external provider or feature will remain available. The driving estimate is freshly requested and is intentionally not stored by Find Me Home.
