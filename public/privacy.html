Find Me Home privacy notice

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Find Me Home keeps household and candidate-plot records in your browser and synchronizes them only with devices that join your household. The driving-time feature is optional and runs only when you press Check drive time.

Driving-time request

To calculate a fresh estimate, the app sends the selected plot's latitude and longitude to the Find Me Home Cloudflare Worker. The Worker sends that origin, the fixed Vilnius city-centre destination, and a future departure time to Google Maps Platform Routes API. Google returns an estimated duration and distance. The app does not send household names, notes, listing details, or device credentials with this request.

The Worker does not store the request or response. The browser displays the result for the current page session and does not save or synchronize it. Cloudflare and Google may process network and request data under their own policies.
