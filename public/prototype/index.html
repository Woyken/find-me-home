Five designs for the saved-plots page

Static prototypes with example data. Same functionality on every one: household name, sync state, rename, invite, switch device, Aruodas import setup, the inbox, the visit plan, and the list itself with add / remove from the plan. Each design also changes the words used in the interface. Flip between them with the black pill at the edge of each page or the ← → keys.

  1. 1 · Field notebook

    A surveyor's field book on graph paper. The real parcel outline from the land registry is drawn beside every plot; checks are counted as symbols; orange is spent only on "going to see".

    Words: Plots · Going to see · New from Aruodas · Our search · marked areas

  2. 2 · Roadside sign

    Phone-first. Each plot is a "for sale" board nailed to a post, price loudest. The 13 checks collapse into one traffic light, the visit toggle is a thumb switch, navigation is a bottom tab bar.

    Words: Land · Trip · Fresh · Go see it? · On the trip

  3. 3 · Ledger

    The comparison sheet. Dense sortable table, every check visible as a strip of 13 cells, €/are computed, quick filters, totals row. Best on a laptop; stacks on a phone.

    Words: Listings · Visit list · Inbox · Household

  4. 4 · Postcards

    The fridge door. Photo-first postcards, a pushpin when a place is on the weekend plan, a rubber stamp once visited, handwritten margin notes. Checks become weather: sunny, cloudy, stormy.

    Words: Places · Weekend plan · New postcards · Pin for the weekend

  5. 5 · Map desk

    The map is the page. A live map of every located plot beside a margin of notes; click either side and the other answers. Coral marks the route. Plots without a location gather at the bottom.

    Words: Saved plots · Route · Unsorted · Add to route
Things worth reacting to when you look:
  • Which vocabulary reads best to someone who has never seen the app.
  • Indicators instead of text: parcel outlines (1), traffic light (2), 13-cell strip (3), weather (4), chips + map colour (5).
  • Where the household controls live: inline settings link (1, 4, 5), gear icon (2), "Manage" in a top bar (3).
  • Import setup as a 3-step sheet everywhere; the wording differs slightly per design.
